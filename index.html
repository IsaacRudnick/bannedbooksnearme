About the data

A ban here means a school district or a state removed a book from school libraries or classrooms, or limited who may read it, as one of the sources below reports.

    Book covers and links come from Open Library. Library data comes from the Institute of Museum and Library Services. School district data comes from the National Center for Education Statistics.
